Revenue Streams

Premium Subscriptions

"Best Speaking Premium": Tiered monthly/annual fees for extra perks like higher-value coupons and early event access

Partner Commissions

5% transaction fee or flat referral fee for each redeemed coupon from partner venues

Marketplace Fees

Small service-matching commission on tutoring, babysitting, and other community services

Sponsored Content

Event sponsorships and featured listings: paid promotions for partners within the app feed

Branded Content

Optional, non-intrusive banner space for language-learning brands and complementary services

Revenue Timeline

Short Term (0-6 months)

Partner commissions and featured listings as initial cash flow once first 10-20 venues onboard

Medium Term (6-18 months)

Verified-Member subscriptions drive MRR once the active user base hits 5k-10k; marketplace fees kick in as services gain traction

Long Term (18+ months)

Sustainable revenue mix from all five streams with premium subscriptions becoming the primary growth driver
